— How it works

The whole thing, in four steps. None of them weird.

1

20-min intro call

You tell me where you're going (or not), when, who, and a budget you'd consider spending. I tell you whether I'm the right fit.

2

Planning fee

$300–$2,500 depending on trip complexity. Locks in your dates and gets me building. Refundable through the first proposal if it's not what you wanted.

3

Proposal & iterate

2–3 options with real numbers, real itineraries, real reasoning. We refine. Once you're in, you book through my platform at the same rate Expedia would charge.

You go. I stay awake.

WhatsApp me if a flight cancels, a hotel's overbooked, or you want a restaurant rec in Tokyo at 11pm local. I'm the human in the loop.
