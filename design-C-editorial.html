— Chapter 01 / The position

The hotel pays the commission. You arrive to upgrades.

Same rate as Booking.com. Different reception. The whole pitch in one sentence — though we will, naturally, write several thousand more.

There is a generation of people now hitting their peak travel years — honeymoons, family weeks, milestone celebrations, business travel — who never had a relationship with a travel advisor. Their parents had advisors. They have Expedia.

The arithmetic, plainly: hotels publish a rate. Whether you book on Booking.com, Expedia, or through me, that rate is identical. The difference is what's waiting in the room.

Through Fora's preferred-partner status across Virtuoso, Four Seasons, Belmond, Rosewood, Hyatt Privé, and the rest, our bookings layer on a confirmed room upgrade, daily breakfast for two, $100–$200 in hotel credits, early check-in, late check-out, and a welcome amenity — all paid by the hotel from its marketing budget, none by you.

That's the entire pitch. Same price. Better arrival.
