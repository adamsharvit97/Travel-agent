## Caveats — read these before you trust the numbers

1. **These are simulated calls, not real prospects.** They project what the funnel yields if these exact conversion rates hold against real buyers of the same type. To hit $105K Y1 actual you need ~60–80 cold contacts converting to ~28 discovery calls converting at these rates.

2. **The probabilities are mine.** A more pessimistic analyst would cut all conditional yeses by 20% and land at $80K. A more optimistic one (the founder, mid-pitch enthusiasm) would push to $130K. Don't anchor too hard on the headline.

3. **Time-to-close matters.** Half of these conditional yeses take 14-60 days to actually sign. Some won't close until Y2. The $105K Y1 number assumes a clean Q1-Q2 close on the conditionals.

4. **The Society tier doesn't exist yet.** Lieberman, Whitmore, and Mateo's Society-track revenue assumes it launches Q3 2026 and they convert. If it doesn't ship, those three numbers degrade ~50%.

5. **Fora's split applies to commissions.** The "Comm" column above is GROSS commission. Net to advisor is 70% (year one) → 80% (at $300K sold) → 90% (at $2M sold). The math above hasn't applied the split — treat net Y1 advisor take as roughly 90-95% of the gross figures shown (because membership and design fees flow direct, commissions are net 70%).

6. **Member usage drives commission yield.** Light members at $99/mo who don't actually use the desk capture little commission. The numbers above assume active usage. If 20% of Light members go dormant after 90 days, deflate Light-tier commission by ~30%.

## What this means for the Y5 business plan

The Base Case in the original financial model projected $171K Y5 net take-home from ~18 active clients. **This cohort of 28 simulated calls, if real, generates Y5 retained revenue of $85K — half of Base Case.** That means:

- A single cohort of this quality covers ~50% of Y5. The other 50% comes from additional cohorts in Y2-Y3, plus retention compounding.
- **To hit Base Case, the founder needs roughly two cohorts of this conversion quality per year, indexed to 28 real qualified calls each.** That's ~56 qualified calls/year, ~5/month, which is realistic if the LinkedIn Journal and referral engine work as designed.
- **To hit Bull Case ($1.5M+ Y5), the founder needs to land ~2 Lakshmi-scale or Lieberman-scale accounts AND build the Society tier.** The current cohort has 4 latent Society candidates (Lieberman, Whitmore, Mateo, Caroline), which is more than enough seed if the tier ships in Q3.
