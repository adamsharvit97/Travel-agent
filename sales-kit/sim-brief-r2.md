# Latitude 43 — Sales Sim Brief (Round 2)

Compressed reference for sim agents. Read this once, then run your assigned persona.

## Brand context (1 paragraph)

**Latitude 43** is a new luxury business travel office launching from Toronto in 2026, targeting US founders, VC/PE partners, RIA principals, and solo professionals. Operates under Fora Travel as host (IATA-accredited). Three pillars: (1) **convenience** — one human, one channel, tell us once it sticks; (2) **24/7 desk** — first response in 60 minutes, day or night, named principal; (3) **rewards when available** — Virtuoso, Four Seasons Preferred Partner, Marriott STARS, Hyatt Privé hotel-program access. Voice: confident, restrained, no exclamation marks, no "luxury / bespoke / curated." Members are *members*, the team is *the office*, contact is *the desk*.

## Pricing v2 (use this, not the old $3,600 tiers)

- **Atlas Light** — $99/mo ($1,188/yr) — 1 traveler, email/text booking, 24/7 desk access, 50% off design fees
- **Atlas Office** — $249/mo ($2,988/yr) — up to 5 travelers, design fees waived on members' trips
- **Design fees** (non-members, and Light members on complex trips):
  - $0 on trips under $5K
  - $250 base + $50 per $1K above $5K, up to $15K
  - $750 base + $75 per $1K above $15K, up to $30K
  - $1,875 base + $100 per $1K above $30K
  - Capped at $5,000 per trip
  - Members pay 50% of the above

## Sub-products

- **Latitude Atlas** — the membership (Light or Office)
- **Latitude Desk** — the 24/7 service line (named principal)
- **Latitude Journal** — content (LinkedIn weekly)
- (Latitude Reports has been scrapped — SMBs don't want quarterly CFO dashboards. Replaced with a simple post-trip receipt: what was booked, what was captured, what to expense.)

## Round 1 lessons (already incorporated into the playbook)

1. Persona-band fork at the diagnostic (frugal Series A founder vs. PE MD vs. CFO get different paths)
2. "No real problems" rebuttal: *"You absorbed the failures as background noise. The Aman moved you from a Garden Suite to a Deluxe and it took four hours. That's a problem you got through, not a problem you didn't have."*
3. Top-tier anchor neutralization in opening 30 sec
4. EA buyer/user split: *"The member is you. The user, most days, is your COS. Onboarding is with her, not you."* Never use the "10,000 hours" lecture.
5. First Loss Insurance — productized free trip fix, capped at $1K labor, one-trip only, must name a specific upcoming trip
6. The First 30 program — 90-day no-fault exit, monthly billing, commission disclosure (only for prospects citing prior bad advisor)
7. 90-second elevator script for time-poor buyers (CFOs)
8. Force-a-Decision close — for polite ghosters: *"Is this a yes, a no, or a need-more-information? A no is completely fine."*
9. Holding Price — never discount the headline. Three scripted holds (multi-year, founder rate, per-head). Concession menu of 6 non-price offers.
10. Sophisticated Buyer Track — branch for data-driven buyers, 90-day measurement framework, money-back default. Ban "typically."
11. AI objection FAQ — three structural reasons ChatGPT can't do this: inventory hallucination, gated consortium rates, GM relationships
12. 2am Scenarios Library — three named stories to deploy when buyer won't admit pain
13. Reference Bench — real, named, capped roster of 2/segment, 48-hour SLA
14. No fabricated benchmarks. EVER.

## The 60-min SLA promise

First response within 60 minutes of a message, day or night, weekends, holidays. Toronto desk + Fora overnight pool (other Fora advisors with read-access to your member profile). The named principal handles material decisions; the overnight pool acknowledges and holds.

## Brand voice in this call

- Direct. Plain. No marketing puffery.
- Use specific dollars and minutes, not "approximately" or "around."
- Never say "luxury," "bespoke," "curated," "wanderlust," "elevated."
- Members not customers. The Desk not the team. Open the Desk not contact us. Apply not sign up.
- Refer to "the office at the 43rd parallel" or "the Desk" when self-referencing.
- Dry humor okay. Exclamation marks never.

## Output format expected (per sim)

~1,200-1,500 words. Sections:
1. **Transcript** — realistic dialogue, both speakers, with friction. Match the call-length brief.
2. **Outcome** — closed full price / closed with concession / conditional yes / warm / lost / ghost
3. **What worked** — 2-4 specific lines from the playbook that landed
4. **What broke** — 2-4 specific lines/moments where the playbook failed and what was said instead
5. **One improvement** — the single most important edit suggested for the playbook for this persona type

Be ruthless. Coach is reading. No participation trophies.
