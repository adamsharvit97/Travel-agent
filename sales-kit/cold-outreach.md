## Cadence Guidance

- Send Tuesday or Wednesday morning, 9:00–10:30 a.m. recipient local time.
- One follow-up at +5 business days, one paragraph, no second pitch — only a single specific insight tied to the recipient's published activity.
- Do not chase past two touches without a referral or a fresh signal.
- Track open rates in a flat spreadsheet, not a CRM, for the first 90 days. The signal is too low-volume for software to add value.
