## THREE IMPROVEMENTS — quoted existing + proposed rewrite

**1. The objection on price**

- *Existing:* *"You wouldn't, and I wouldn't ask you to. Firm is for fifteen travelers. For you specifically — one traveler, twenty-eight trips, four of them high-stakes — you're Principal. Thirty-six hundred a year. Three hundred a month."*
- *Why it underperforms:* Founder corrects Derek's framing ("$36K could buy five years of Navan") by re-tiering him, but never directly addresses the underlying math anxiety. Derek is an engineer — he wants the comparison stated in his unit.
- *Proposed rewrite:* *"You're comparing the wrong tier. Firm is fifteen travelers — that's not you. You're Principal. Thirty-six hundred. Here's the unit comparison an engineer will care about: Navan's per-seat cost for you is roughly six hundred a year. We're six times that. We are not six times better at booking a Delta seat to Austin. We are infinitely better on the four trips a year where Navan's chat queue is the wrong tool. You're paying the multiple on the trips that matter, not the ones that don't."*

**2. The benchmark question**

- *Existing:* *"Direct comparison on the routine trip? We don't, and I won't claim we do. Navan rebooks a coach seat at three a.m. faster than I can pick up the phone."*
- *Why it underperforms:* Strong opening, then collapses into two probably-fabricated member anecdotes. The honest concession is the strongest move; doubling down with stories undoes it.
- *Proposed rewrite:* *"Direct comparison on the routine trip — we don't beat Navan, and I won't claim we do. Their chat queue rebooks a coach seat at three a.m. faster than I can. On the high-stakes trip the benchmark you should ask for isn't published — it's the SLA in writing. Sixty minutes, day or night, named human. Navan won't put that in a contract. Ask them. If they will, sign with them. If they won't, that's the gap I'm filling."*

**3. The hidden-pain surfacing**

- *Existing:* *"I'd ask: in the last twelve months, has there been a moment where the Navan chat queue felt like the wrong tool? Not a catastrophe. Just — wrong tool."*
- *Why it underperforms:* This worked — Derek surfaced the bot incident. But "wrong tool" is abstract. A sharper question would have surfaced it earlier and at higher specificity, which compounds trust faster.
- *Proposed rewrite:* *"One more question and I'll stop. The chat-and-AI model — Navan, Concur, all of them — is great at confirmation and re-issuing. It is bad at judgment under pressure. When was the last time their bot made a call that you, with thirty seconds, would have made differently? Doesn't have to be a disaster. Just — a call you'd have made differently."*

The persona shipped because Founder held the layer-above frame under direct fire. The deal closes or breaks on whether the comparison sheet, sent Tuesday, holds that same frame in writing. If the sheet leads with "vs. Navan" instead of "on top of Navan," Derek walks.
