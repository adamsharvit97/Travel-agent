# Latitude 43 — Round 6 Sim Brief

## Three offering models being tested

### Model A — v2.0 Original
- **Atlas Light:** $99/mo (1 traveler, basic booking)
- **Atlas Office:** $249/mo (up to 5 active travelers, includes EA Companion Seat)
- **Design fees:** $0 under $5K; escalator $250-$5,000; members 50% off
- **Risk reversal:** First Trip Guarantee

### Model B — Unlimited (recommended pivot)
- **Atlas Light Unlimited:** $189/mo (1 traveler, ALL trip design included, no escalator)
- **Atlas Office Unlimited:** $349/mo (up to 5 active travelers, ALL design included, includes EA Companion Seat)
- **Design fees:** ZERO for members (Unlimited)
- **Risk reversal:** First Trip Guarantee

### Model C — Three-tier SMB ladder
- **Atlas Light Basic:** $99/mo (basic booking, design fee escalator applies)
- **Atlas Light Plus:** $249/mo (booking + design included on member trips)
- **Atlas Office:** $499/mo (up to 5 active travelers, full inclusive)
- **Risk reversal:** First Trip Guarantee (on Plus and Office tiers only)

## Three First Trip Guarantee mechanics (one assigned per sim)

### FTG1 — Month + labor credit
"If your first trip fails on our end, we refund the month's membership AND credit $1,000 of labor toward the rework. One trip, named on the call."

### FTG2 — Full refund + free month + service credit
"If your first trip fails on our end, we refund the month, give you the next month free, AND issue a $500 service credit for a future trip. Maximum trust signal."

### FTG3 — 30/60-day money-back guarantee
"30-day money-back on the membership itself. Doesn't matter why. You decide if it was worth it; if not, you get the month back, no questions."

## Brand context (compressed)

Latitude 43 — Toronto-based travel office hosted under Fora. Targets US founders, VC/PE partners, RIA principals, solo professionals. PHT VA covering US daytime + founder on evenings/weekends/overnights. 60-min SLA. Society H2 2027 (deferred until Round 6 data).

**Banned words:** luxury, bespoke, curated, white-glove, seamless, elevated, wanderlust, concierge.
**Preferred:** members, the desk, apply, open the desk, standing instructions.
**Register:** confessional > triumphalist. Operator-correct.

## Sim output structure (every sim, ~3,200 words)

### Section 1: TRANSCRIPT (~1,500 words)
Realistic dialogue showing the assigned MODEL pricing and FTG mechanic delivered in context. Include moments of friction, real human dynamics, the specific points where the buyer reacts to the model and the FTG.

### Section 2: SALES COACH EVALUATION (~700 words)
Head-of-sales analysis. Outcome. What worked specifically (cite lines). What broke. Three improvements. The most important behavioral change for this rep.

### Section 3: BUSINESS EVALUATOR (~700 words)
McKinsey-trained business analysis. Unit economics implications of the model at this conversion. Strategic positioning learnings. Retention probability. Referral value. The hidden risk.

### Section 4: MODEL + FTG SCORECARD (~300 words)
Tight scoring on:
- Did the price land or stick? (Note specific friction moments)
- Did the FTG mechanic move the buyer? (Was it decisive, helpful, or ignored?)
- Would the buyer have bought under a DIFFERENT model? (Compare to the other two if relevant)
- Net read: which model variant best fit this specific buyer/stage?

## The five sales-cycle stages and personas

### Stage 1 — Cold inbound discovery
Buyer: Aaron-equivalent. 14-person agency founder, $70K T&E, replied to cold email two days ago. Pre-deal, no relationship.

### Stage 2 — Atlas Office onboarding with EA in the room
Buyer: Maria-equivalent. 28-person boutique RIA managing partner, $340M AUM, just signed. EA Patricia (8 years tenured) on the call.

### Stage 3 — Live IROP at 2am
Buyer: Daniel-equivalent. Atlas Office member, 6 weeks in, second trip. Series B founder flying to a critical pitch meeting. Delta flight cancels at 2:30am EST.

### Stage 4 — Mid-year low-usage check-in
Buyer: Sarah-equivalent. M&A boutique partner 6 months in, used Latitude for 4 of 16 trips, EA Sophie keeps defaulting to Concur. Retention risk.

### Stage 5 — Price objection sim
Buyer: NEW. SMB founder loves the pitch but objects to the entry price ("$189 / $249 / $99 — I wasn't expecting that"). How does the founder hold price under genuine pushback?
