# Latitude 43 — Sales Sim Brief (Round 3)

## Brand context (compressed)

Latitude 43 is a Toronto-based travel office, hosted under Fora Travel (IATA-accredited). Targets US founders, VC/PE partners, RIA principals, family office principals, and solo professionals. Three pillars: **convenience** (one human, one channel), **24/7 desk** (60-min SLA, named principal), **rewards when available** (Virtuoso, Four Seasons Preferred Partner, Marriott STARS, Hyatt Privé). Voice: confident, restrained, no exclamation marks. **Banned words: luxury, bespoke, curated, wanderlust, white-glove, seamless, elevated.** Members not customers. The Desk not the team. Apply not sign up.

## Pricing v2

- **Atlas Light** — $99/mo ($1,188/yr) — 1 traveler, email/text, 24/7 desk, 50% off design fees
- **Atlas Office** — $249/mo ($2,988/yr) — up to 5 travelers, design fees waived on members' trips
- **Society (forthcoming Q3 2026, invite-only, capped at 30)** — $1,000+/mo, dedicated principal, family-layer coverage
- **Design fees** (non-members, and Light members on complex trips):
  - $0 under $5K
  - $250 base + $50/$1K from $5K-$15K
  - $750 base + $75/$1K from $15K-$30K
  - $1,875 base + $100/$1K above $30K
  - Capped at $5,000/trip · Members pay 50%

## Round 1 + Round 2 lessons (now in the playbook)

1. **Persona-band fork at diagnostic** — frugal Series A vs PE MD vs CFO vs delegated buyer get different paths
2. **"No real problems" rebuttal** — *"You absorbed the failures as background noise."*
3. **Top-tier anchor neutralization** in opening 30 sec for HNW: *"You're talking Principal — $300/mo, not the Firm tier."*
4. **EA buyer/user split** — *"The member is you. The user, most days, is your COS."* Never the "10,000 hours" lecture.
5. **First Loss Insurance — offered EARLY, not as save** — capped at $1K labor, one trip, must be named on the call
6. **The First 30 program** for burned-before — 90-day no-fault, monthly billing, commission disclosure
7. **90-second elevator script** for CFOs and time-poor: pricing + tier + 3 differentiators
8. **Force-a-Decision close** — *"Is this a yes, a no, or a need-more-information?"*
9. **Holding Price** — never discount. Three rehearsed holds (multi-year, founder rate, per-head). Concession menu of 6 non-price offers
10. **Sophisticated Buyer Track** — branch for data-driven buyers, 90-day measurement framework, money-back default, ban "typically"
11. **AI / ChatGPT objection FAQ** — three structural reasons it can't do this
12. **2am Scenarios Library** — three named stories to deploy when buyer won't admit pain
13. **Reference Bench** — 2 per segment, pre-consented, 48-hour SLA
14. **No fabricated benchmarks** — "I'm new. I don't have a public benchmark. Two members will take your call this week."
15. **"Layer above"** framing for Navan/TMC defenders — never compete with the existing system
16. **"Disqualify up"** to Society for HNW — *"$249 is the wrong product for you"* flips dynamic
17. **Spouse line for bootstrapped founders** — *"$99 isn't really for you. It's for Sunday night with [partner]."*
18. **Multi-EA coordination layer** — *"We sell the seam between your two EAs."*
19. **Family-first reframe for married buyers** — *"The person who has to be happy in 6 months is not you, it's [spouse]."*
20. **Multilingual backup principal** — pre-staged for Latam/Iberian/Asian families
21. **"Service contract on a scientific instrument"** for technical buyers (biotech, engineers)
22. **The post-yes onboarding hook** — book the EA's onboarding call before ending the founder's call
23. **Atlas Family Office variant** — rolling roster of 5 active from a pool of up to 20, default-off marketing clause
24. **The "concierge" word is banned** — founders never use it; marketers do
25. **Confessional register, not triumphalist** — match the LinkedIn voice of buyers who admit travel costs

## Brand voice in this call

- Direct. Plain. No marketing puffery. Use specific dollars and minutes, not "approximately."
- Never say: luxury / bespoke / curated / white-glove / elevated / wanderlust / concierge
- Members. The Desk. The office. Apply. Open the desk.
- Dry humor okay. Exclamation marks never.

## Output format (per sim)

~1,200-1,500 words.

1. **Transcript** — realistic dialogue, both speakers, with friction. Match the call brief.
2. **Outcome** — closed full price / closed with concession / conditional yes / warm / lost / ghost
3. **What worked** — 2-4 specific lines that landed
4. **What broke** — 2-4 moments where the playbook failed
5. **One improvement** — the single most important edit for this persona type

Be ruthless. Coach is reading. No participation trophies.

## CRITICAL FOR ROUND 3

These sims test EDGE CASES not covered before — renewals, win-backs, EA gatekeepers, multi-decision-maker calls, hostile-referral, spouse-as-buyer, press-inbound, one-off project buyers. The playbook will break in new places. That's the point. Find the gaps.
