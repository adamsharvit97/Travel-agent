# Latitude 43 — Sales Sim Brief (Round 4 — COLD LEADS)

## CRITICAL FRAMING

These are COLD leads. Not warm intros. Not referrals. Not prospects who saw the founder in the press. These are people who:
- Half-remember (or don't remember) replying to a cold email
- Took the call out of politeness, curiosity, or because their EA scheduled it
- Have NO emotional buy-in to the brand
- Default to skepticism, dismissiveness, distraction, or politeness-as-deflection
- Will NOT close on the call in most cases
- Many will walk early, ghost, or say "send me materials" as a kill

**The point of Round 4 is to find where the playbook BREAKS under cold conditions.** A 100% conversion rate in R3 means the personas were too warm. Real cold conversion looks like 5-15% first-call close. We need to see:
- Where the diagnostic dies because the prospect won't engage
- Which playbook lines get rejected when there's no trust equity
- What the founder does when the prospect is openly bored/hostile/distracted
- How the founder exits cleanly when a deal is dead — the "walking away with dignity" play

## Cold-lead realities to embed in each sim

1. **The prospect's opening line is rarely "tell me about Latitude."** It's "who is this?" or "I have 10 minutes" or "remind me why I agreed to this"
2. **The diagnostic will die at question 2** if the prospect doesn't trust the founder yet. The "what's your worst trip" question only works after credibility is established
3. **The reframe will not land** if delivered too early — cold prospects need 60-90 seconds of competence-signaling before they'll listen to a value frame
4. **The Force-a-Decision close** has different outcomes on cold leads: many will say "no, not now" rather than "send materials" — and that's a CLEANER outcome than a polite ghost
5. **Most cold sims should end with a "no" or a soft "maybe call me in 6 months"** — not with a sale. The founder's job is to leave the door open without being needy.

## Round 1-3 playbook (still applicable)

All 25 lessons from prior rounds. But COLD calls strip away the warmth that made some moves work. Test which lessons survive cold conditions and which need cold-specific variants.

## Output format

~1,200-1,500 words. Same structure as R3:
1. **Transcript** — realistic dialogue with COLD-lead friction (interruptions, side conversations, distractions, "let me jump on real quick" energy)
2. **Outcome** — explicit. Most will be: walked, ghosted, polite no, soft maybe, send-materials-dead. A few might be conditional yes. Maybe one closes.
3. **What worked** — even in a no, what did the founder do well?
4. **What broke** — where did the playbook fail under cold conditions?
5. **One improvement** — for THIS persona archetype specifically

**Be brutal. Most of these are LOSSES.** That's the honest truth of cold outreach. A coach reviewing this wants the failure data more than wins.
