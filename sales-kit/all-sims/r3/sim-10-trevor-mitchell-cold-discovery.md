# Outcome

**Conditional yes** — pending reference call inside 48 hours. First Loss Insurance named (Frankfurt June trip). Lauren onboarding gated as the real close.

# What worked

1. **Re-reading the original email anecdote in the first 60 seconds** — exactly the hook the brief required. Trevor's "huh, okay, yeah I remember" was the unlock.
2. **"You said that, I didn't"** after the Lauren-six-hours line — let Trevor own the pain instead of being sold it. Dry, restrained, on-voice.
3. **Disclosing "I'm new, 40 members, 14 months" at minute 11** — pre-empted the discovery he'd otherwise make alone later. Converted a future objection into present credibility.
4. **Force-a-Decision close with three named options** — refused the "send me a PDF" exit. Got a specific information request instead of a vague "let me think."

# What broke

1. **Worst-case design fee math was delivered too fast.** $1,125 → $562 in one breath, no pause for Trevor to absorb. A COO doing mental math on a phone call needs three seconds. He covered with a joke; a different persona would have stalled there.
2. **The Lauren objection arrived unprompted at minute 14 and almost derailed the close.** Should have been surfaced *by the founder* at minute 6, not waited for. The EA-buyer/user split is in the playbook (lesson #4) and wasn't deployed proactively.
3. **"Two members in roughly your shape"** — vague. Should have said "a 60-person SaaS COO and a 90-person fintech CFO" up front. Trevor had to ask for the fintech one specifically. Specificity should be free.
4. **No commercial mechanism for the sales-team 50 trips.** Founder waved them off as "stays on Concur" — correct, but left $50K-$80K of annual trip volume undiscussed. A delegated-buyer COO will revisit that gap in month three and feel undersold.

# One improvement

**Surface Lauren in minute 4, not minute 14.** For any COO-with-EA persona, the EA isn't a late objection — she's the entire deal. The script should be: *"Before I tell you what I think this is, who runs your travel today? Because if it's an EA, this call is really a pre-call for the call I'm going to have with her, and I should know her name before I pitch you."* That single move converts the EA from a risk into a co-buyer and shortens the call by four minutes.
