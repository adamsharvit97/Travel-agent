## OUTCOME
**Closed full price** — Atlas Office $249/mo, with a live-trial trip seeded before the contract executes. Society flagged for Q3 re-up conversation.

## WHAT WORKED
1. **"The journalist did good work. Glad you read it."** — Matter-of-fact press acknowledgment. Not needy, not dismissive. Passed the first test in eight seconds.
2. **Named the two competing firms by name and gave them real credit** — Marcus came in armed with "which one of you is honest." Conceding Fifty Grand's leisure depth and Boundless's tech roadmap bought every other claim afterward.
3. **"It is. With a named backup principal."** — Refused to weasel on the one-person-shop question. Then gave the real version.
4. **"Disqualify up" to Society done correctly** — "$249 is the right product today" reframed Marcus from skeptic to buyer. Combined with EA carve-out for Carol, removed two objections at once.

## WHAT BROKE
1. **The "low triple digits" capacity number** — Honest answer, but the lack of a memorized number in a press-inbound call is a small credibility tax. A real founder should know this cold. Buyer let it slide; a harder buyer wouldn't.
2. **Society pitch was reactive, not proactive** — Marcus had to ask. On a press-inbound call where the article hinted at Society, the founder should surface it within the first five minutes and use it as the framing device for the cap discussion.
3. **The 60-min SLA → free-month remedy** — Strong, but offered defensively in response to the #18-on-the-list fear. Should have been volunteered with the SLA itself in minute two. Reactive SLA-with-teeth reads as save; proactive reads as confidence.
4. **No commission disclosure surfaced unprompted** — Article-driven buyers are testing for hidden economics. The hotel-commission line landed late and only because Marcus asked "what's the catch." Should be standard in the press-inbound diagnostic.

## ONE IMPROVEMENT
**Build a 90-second press-inbound opener that front-loads the three things the article couldn't:** (1) the exact current member count and the cap math, (2) the SLA-with-teeth remedy, (3) the commission economics. Press readers arrive with the marketing version already in their head — the founder's only job in the first two minutes is to replace it with the operator's version, unprompted. Waiting for the buyer to ask is leaving margin on the table and forfeits the "honest operator" frame that the entire press-inbound motion depends on.
