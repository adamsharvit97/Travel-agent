## Outcome

**CLOSED.** Atlas Office, $249/mo, no annual commitment. ~$3K ARR plus expected design fee revenue on the family trip (~$1K) and hotel commissions on ~$25-40K of bookings annually (~$3-5K). Total expected first-year economics: $7-9K.

## What worked

- **Founder led with price in sentence one.** Yusef relaxed visibly when he got the number inside 15 seconds. The "want to keep going or pressure-test" handoff was the unlock — it gave control to the engineer brain.
- **Unit economics answered honestly.** Saying "hotel commissions subsidize the desk, that's why sub-$99 doesn't exist" passed Yusef's bullshit filter. He's run a P&L. He could smell a margin story that didn't add up.
- **The Navan delineation.** Yusef was going to ask. Pre-empting it with a clean "we're not them, here's the axis" let him categorize Latitude and move on. Engineers need a mental slot for new products.
- **Refusing to oversell at the end.** "If you fly less than 10 segments, Atlas Light" — voluntarily routing him down-tier signaled the founder wasn't optimizing for the bigger sale. That's what closed it.
- **No discovery questions.** Founder skipped the diagnostic entirely. With this archetype, the diagnostic is the prospect doing math out loud while you stay quiet.

## What broke

- **Nothing meaningful broke** — but this is a best-case Yusef. A bad version of this call has Yusef interrupting at second 8 with "I have to jump, send me materials" and the founder never gets to the unit economics. The play is fragile to the prospect's mood.
- **The 60-minute SLA claim** is the soft spot. If Yusef had asked for a reference customer or a screenshot of past response times, the founder would have had to hedge. Worth pre-building a "here's a redacted thread" artifact for the engineer archetype.
- **"We read your policy once, we file it"** is hand-wavy. A CFO will push harder than Yusef did. The founder needs a clearer answer for what "file it" operationally means.

## One improvement for THIS archetype

**Build a 90-second "engineer cold open" template.** Price, product, unit economics, what we're not, in that order, before the prospect can interrupt. Then shut up. For Yusef-types, the only valid discovery is the prospect doing his own math against your numbers. The founder's job is to provide clean inputs and resist the urge to fill silence with brand language. This call worked because the founder didn't say "convenience" or "24/7 desk" as a value prop — he said "$99, here's the SLA, here's the commission math." Engineer brain accepts numbers. It rejects adjectives.
