## Outcome
**Conditional yes.** Atlas Office contingent on COS operational validation. First Loss attached to a named trip (Cape Town, July). Society flagged for Q4.

## What worked
1. **30-second anchor neutralization** — "you're not our average member, I'll skip a chunk of the normal pitch" disarmed the megafund-MD reflex inside the first minute.
2. **EA buyer/user split, reframed up-stack** — "a layer above the three you have, not a fourth person" answered the strongest objection without making him feel sold to.
3. **Aman/Park Hyatt reframe of the Seoul story** — naming the actual mechanism (PMS flag vs. business card in a drawer) rather than asserting "we're better than your relationships."
4. **Honest pricing math** — telling him the $249 is a marker and commission is the revenue. He flagged it as the right move; most operators dodge.

## What broke
1. **Society tier was mentioned slightly too early.** Reg called it out ("don't sell me something that doesn't exist"). It landed because he respects directness, but a tighter version would have held Society until *after* the conditional yes, as a forward-looking gift rather than a pitch element.
2. **Reference bench framing was wobbly** — "PE partner at a $12B fund" is borderline benchmark territory. He didn't push but a sharper version names the reference's role (not fund size) and lets him connect dots himself.
3. **"I cannot prove we deliver at your level until we do"** worked but only because Reg respects candor. With a slightly more skeptical buyer at this altitude that line is a gift to a "then why are we talking" reply. Needs a stronger bridge.

## One improvement
**Add a "Multi-EA Coordination Brief" as a deliverable for the COS follow-up call.** The playbook currently treats EA onboarding as a single-COS handoff. For the megafund household with 3+ assistants, the *single most important artifact* is a one-page document that says, in plain English: *which EA emails the Desk for what, what they stop doing, what the master itinerary looks like, who owns the family-layer overlay.* This is the wedge that converts "fourth person" objection into "coordination layer" acceptance. Without it, the COS call is a vibes check; with it, the COS has something concrete to bring back to her principal. Build the template.
