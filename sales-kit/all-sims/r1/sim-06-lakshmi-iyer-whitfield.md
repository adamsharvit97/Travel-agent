## Outcome

**Conditional yes.** Atlas Office at $249/mo + per-conference design fees, contingent on Priya's read of the SuperReturn run-of-show. Estimated annual: $12K. Start date January 2026 if Priya signs off.

## What worked

1. **Opening reframe under 30 seconds**: "the coordination layer between Priya and NYC, not a pitch to replace either of them" — neutralized the top objection before she raised it.
2. **Two diagnostic questions before pitching** — peer-to-peer move, made her do the talking, surfaced the "twenty scattered minutes" pain in her own words.
3. **Honest SuperReturn answer** ("I have not personally run that week, Fora has") — the one moment she explicitly flagged as buying signal. Refusing to bluff was the trade.
4. **First Loss Insurance pointed at SuperReturn, not the side trip** — the "brave or stupid" line landed as dry humor, matched her register.

## What broke

1. **"That's a Notion page with extra steps"** — the rebuttal was okay but slow. Should have led with the artifact-vs-work distinction in the original pitch and pre-empted it. Lost about 40 seconds.
2. **Pricing range was wide** ("$9K to $15K"). For a $4B fund-of-funds Partner this is rounding error, but for a sophisticated buyer it reads as imprecise. Should have given a single number for a modeled SuperReturn week and let her extrapolate.
3. **Named-principal handoff at six months** — introduced a future friction point unprompted. She didn't push on it, but it was a gift to a future objection. Should have answered "me" and stopped.
4. **"Brave or stupid on my part"** — landed because she has dry humor, but with a different LP this is the breathless overshare she would have walked on. Risky read.

## One improvement

**Build a Conference Week Pricing Sheet** — SuperReturn, IPEM, ILPA Summit, Milken, Davos — each with a single modeled price for a "standard partner trip" (hotel block + car stack + 8-10 dinners + one side trip). When a sophisticated LP buyer asks cost shape, you quote one number per week, not a range. The range reads amateur. The single number reads like someone who has priced this before. This is the highest-leverage edit for the LP/PE Partner persona band.
