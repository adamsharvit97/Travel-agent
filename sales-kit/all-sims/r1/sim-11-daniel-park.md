## Outcome
**Conditional yes** — verbal commit to Atlas Light at $99/mo pending reference call with a Skadden peer inside 48 hours. Start date: June.

## What worked
1. **"The firm vs. you" frame in the opening 90 seconds** — refused to position as TMC replacement. "A personal layer that sits next to it." This was the unlock; without it Daniel walks.
2. **Casual Big Law name-drop, capped and unnamed** — "two at Skadden actually, though I won't name them on a cold call." Signaled social proof without violating discretion. He asked for the reference, which is the buying signal.
3. **Honest answer on expensing** — "at most firms, no… it's on your AmEx." Killed the objection by refusing to dodge it. A $1,800/hr partner respects directness over a workaround.
4. **Specific dollar examples on consortium value** — Four Seasons George V breakfast + $100 credit + upgrade. Made the $99 feel like a rounding error, which is the right frame for this persona.

## What broke
1. **"Your secretary becomes the auditor" line landed flat** — Daniel didn't react. For a partner who delegates everything, reframing his secretary's role mid-call may feel presumptuous. Should have just said "she stops being the broker" and stopped.
2. **The "math doesn't matter, you stop being the project manager of your own weekends" line is close but slightly preachy** — Daniel's a guy who'd cut you off if you started lecturing. Got away with it but barely. Should be shorter: "the math is a wash; the reason is your Sundays."
3. **Didn't deploy the 2am Scenarios Library cleanly** — invoked the Peninsula Chicago story he volunteered, but didn't follow with a named, dramatized Latitude resolution story to match. Missed the chance to make the SLA tangible.
4. **Sixty-minute SLA explanation was defensive** — "Resolution depends on the airline" was correct but the founder over-explained. For this persona, state the SLA, stop, let him push if he cares.

## One improvement
**Add a "Big Law partner" sub-track to the persona-band fork.** This buyer is neither founder nor PE MD nor CFO — he's a delegated-travel professional whose firm owns the booking infrastructure. The single most important move is reframing the product as a *personal layer next to the firm's TMC*, not as a TMC at all. The playbook should script this fork explicitly: opening 30 seconds must include "we're not replacing your firm's TMC, we're the layer next to it for the trips it can't or won't handle." Without that, the buyer correctly says "the firm handles my travel" and the call dies in the first two minutes.
