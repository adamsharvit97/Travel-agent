## Outcome
**Conditional yes — Office tier, contingent on Marina's onboarding call.** ~95% close probability. Monthly billing, in the First 30 cohort, First Loss Insurance on a named trip.

## What worked
1. **"The two-forty-nine is for Marina"** — the explicit reframe he was waiting for. He couldn't ask for it himself; the founder named it and he took it.
2. **EA buyer/user split delivered verbatim** — "The member is you. The user, most days, is her." Landed because it acknowledged Marina without diminishing her.
3. **2am scenario deployed cold** — the Aman Tokyo Garden Suite story used as a *specific* memory anchor, not a hypothetical. Pulled out a pause, which is the tell with this persona.
4. **Consortium credentials as the un-replicable moat** — Virtuoso/FSPP/STARS/Privé reframed Marina's gap as structural, not skill. He stopped defending her.

## What broke
1. **"Is this the part where you tell me about an app"** — the founder recovered with dry humor, but the app got mentioned at all because the buyer raised it. Playbook should preempt: kill the app reference in the opening 30 seconds for old-school buyers, same slot as the top-tier anchor neutralization.
2. **The forty-five-trips math claim** — "you'd clear the spread on two complex trips" is directionally true but unsourced in the moment. James didn't push, but a sharper buyer would have. Need a one-line worked example ready.
3. **"Pressman's on Office"** — disclosed a referenceable member's tier without explicit permission. Risky. The recovery ("I won't characterize his usage") was fine but the original line shouldn't have left the founder's mouth.
4. **No price hold attempted** — he accepted the $249 without negotiation, but the founder didn't pre-arm. If he'd pushed, no scripted hold was queued.

## One improvement
**Add a "Senior Operator + Long-Tenured EA" branch to the playbook.** This persona has no admittable pain and won't take a pitch aimed at him — every value claim has to be redirected to the EA without making the EA sound replaceable. The pivot phrase ("the two-forty-nine is for Marina, not you") is the entire close for this segment and should be the named move, not improvised. Pair it with a standing rule: **never offer Office first; let him hear Light, react to the price, then frame Office as the gift to the EA.** That sequence is what made him ask for it instead of being sold it.
