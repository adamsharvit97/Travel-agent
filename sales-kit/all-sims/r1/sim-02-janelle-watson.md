## Outcome

**Closed full price** — Atlas Light, $99/mo, monthly billing, onboarding booked Thursday 10am ET. First Loss Insurance attached to next London trip. Expected signature within 24 hours, well inside the 14-day window.

## What worked

1. **"Do the math out loud. I'll wait."** — Forced Janelle to sell herself on the $42/trip framing rather than Ari pitching ROI at her. She arrived at "obvious" on her own.
2. **Persona-band fork at the diagnostic** — three crisp questions in 90 seconds, no preamble. Honored the 15-minute clock and signaled this wasn't a generic pitch.
3. **"You're not the smallest fish — you're the cleanest fit."** — Reframed the solo objection from "am I too small" to "you're actually the ideal member." Specific, not flattering.
4. **First Loss Insurance landed on the Connaught story.** Naming the trip ("point it at the next London trip") made it concrete instead of a brochure feature. This was the close.

## What broke

1. **"Latitude 43 is a travel office under Fora"** — early Fora mention before Janelle asked. She didn't care, but with a different persona this could read as hiding behind a host. Should have been on-demand, not volunteered.
2. **"Most of our members are founders or solo principals"** — soft fabrication. The brand is new; there's no real population yet. Janelle didn't push, but a sharper buyer would have asked "how many" and the answer would have been ugly. Cut.
3. **"The other catch is on complex trips…"** — design-fee disclosure was correct but came in heavy and slightly defensive. Could have been a one-liner: "Hotel-only is free. Complex multi-stop trips have a design fee; you'll see maybe three a year." Two sentences, not five.
4. **"It's how we put skin in the game on month one"** — borderline marketing puffery. Brand voice bans that register. Should have been: "We eat the cost. One trip, your choice."

## One improvement

**For the solo-professional band, lead with First Loss Insurance earlier — by minute 5, not minute 11.** Janelle's pain was specific and recent (the Sunday overbook, the Connaught walk). Every minute spent on consortium-rate mechanics and overnight-pool architecture before naming the insurance was a minute the deal could have died to a calendar interruption. The playbook treats First Loss as a closing instrument; for solo buyers with fresh burn scars it's the *opening* instrument. Move it up.
