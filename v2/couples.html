Why doing it yourself is harder than it looks

A short, honest list.

  • Fourteen hotel options on Booking. None of the photos look like the real room.
  • The restaurants that are actually worth it need a thirty-day reservation. Nobody told you.
  • The neighbourhood that photographs well is twenty-five minutes from anything.
  • The €1,400/night hotel is sometimes worse than the €700/night one. You can't tell from the listings.
  • You have a full-time job. You don't have twenty hours.
