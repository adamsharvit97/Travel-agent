The Approach

A travel advisor used to be something only people of a certain age had. We think that's wrong.

i.

Same rate. Better stay.

The hotels you're already considering — booked at the same nightly rate you'd find online, except you arrive to a room upgrade, daily breakfast for two, a $100 food & beverage credit, and a 4pm checkout. This is not a discount. It is access.

ii.

An itinerary that's actually been thought about.

We will not send you a Google Doc with eighty bullet points. You'll get a short, opinionated plan: the restaurants worth booking sixty days ahead, the museum it's okay to skip, the half-day you should leave open on purpose.

iii.

A real number to call when something breaks.

Your flight is cancelled in Lisbon. The hotel doesn't honour the upgrade. The restaurant lost your reservation. You text us. We fix it. That's the entire job, after the booking is made.

Why Us, Not Expedia

A short, honest comparison.

Booking the trip yourself

  • You pay the public rate. No room upgrade. No breakfast. No credits.
  • The hotel doesn't know who you are when you walk in.
  • Customer service is a 40-minute hold with a call centre in Manila.
  • If you get bumped, you get bumped.
  • Time to plan a two-week trip yourself: 12–20 hours.

Booking with us

  • Same public rate. Room upgrade, breakfast, $100 credit, late checkout.
  • Hotel knows your name, your anniversary, your champagne.
  • Customer service is a person who knows you, by text.
  • If you get bumped, we already've called the GM.
  • Time for you to plan: 90 minutes total, including the trip.
