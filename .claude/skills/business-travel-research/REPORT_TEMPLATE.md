## Style rules

- Specific over general: "8-minute walk to Marunouchi" beats "centrally located"
- Every number (rate, transfer time, fare) carries a currency/unit and a source
- Conflicting sources: state both ("FlyerTalk reports the lounge closed in 2025; the hotel site still lists it — confirm before booking")
- Write for someone who will book within 48 hours, not for a tourism brochure
