A note from the principal

Welcome to Atlas.

Thank you for joining us. There are not many of you, and that is deliberate. A small membership is the only way the desk can know you well enough to be useful in the moments that matter.

What you have bought, more than the booking, is a standing right to call. Trips you can plan yourself. The desk exists for the half hour between an itinerary breaking and a board meeting starting. For the night the hotel walks you. For the morning you need to fly somewhere and have not yet figured out how. We have built the firm around those moments.

The next two weeks are about building your travel profile. Once we know how you travel, the desk becomes faster every month. By the end of the year, most requests will take a sentence on your end and an afternoon on ours.

One ask. When something is wrong, tell us early. We would rather rebuild a calm trip in the morning than rescue a broken one at midnight. We can do both. The first is better for everyone.

Scope

What we book and what we do not.

Clarity here protects the relationship. The desk works best when the boundary is known.

What we book

  • Commercial air, paid and award
  • Private aviation through partners
  • Hotels, all categories, with amenities
  • Ground transport, named drivers, fixers
  • Rail, including private compartments
  • Visas, ETAs, entry clearances
  • Travel insurance placement
  • Disruption handling and claims
  • Itinerary documents, daily briefs

What we do not

  • Restaurant reservations under our own name
  • Personal shopping or errands
  • Childcare or nanny coordination
  • Medical bookings outside travel
  • Real estate or property
  • Tax or immigration advice
  • Concert or sporting event ticketing
  • Anything we cannot do well

When we cannot do something, we will say so and recommend who can. The desk keeps a vetted list of specialists for everything outside our scope.

Confidentiality and data

Your file stays with the desk.

Member files are held in encrypted storage, accessed only by the principal and the advisors assigned to your account. We do not sell, share, or aggregate member data. Loyalty numbers and document images are stored separately from itinerary records. On request, we will export your file or destroy it. The full data handling policy is available at policy.latitude43.travel.
