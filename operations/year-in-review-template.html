Notable trips

Four moments from the year.

March
NYC roadshow, fourteen LP meetings, four days.
The Mark, Park Suite, all four nights. A standing breakfast table held at 07:00 at the lobby restaurant. Blacklane on standby from 07:45 to 19:00 every day. Two days extended by 6 hours when the Greenwich meeting ran long; the hotel held the suite and the driver swapped without prompting. The Mark concierge sourced a missing cufflink between meetings on day two. Trip closed on schedule.
YYZ → JFK 4 nights The Mark
June
Family Office Forum, Geneva, five nights.
Mandarin Oriental Geneva, executive suite upgrade at check-in. The forum overlapped with two private dinners at the Beau-Rivage and Domaine de Châteauvieux. A driver from Limousine Service Genève worked the full five days. The Forum's last-night dinner was relocated 24 hours out; the desk rebooked four members of your party simultaneously without changing the original transport schedule.
YYZ → ZRH → GVA 5 nights Mandarin Oriental
August
Lake Como, eight nights, personal.
Villa d'Este, lakeside suite. The longest stretch of the year. A boat held standing at the dock through the eight days. Three dinners arranged at properties around the lake — Il Sereno, Passalacqua, Grand Hotel Tremezzo — each at the table the property reserves for arriving guests rather than the floor. The flight back was disrupted at LIN by a 6-hour delay; the desk rebooked through MXP and held a car at the new gate.
YYZ → JFK → LIN 8 nights Villa d'Este
November
Tokyo, six nights, two purposes.
Aman Tokyo, three nights, then a transfer to the Mandarin for the back half to be closer to the Marunouchi office. The transition was handled with no overlap in luggage or transport. A standing 06:30 table at Aman for the pre-meeting briefings. The desk arranged a same-day suit alteration at Isetan when a jacket needed taking in before the board dinner.
YYZ → HND 6 nights Aman · Mandarin
