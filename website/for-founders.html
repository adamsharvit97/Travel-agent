Built for · Founders

You are not the one who should be on hold with United.

For founders of seed-through-Series-B companies. Investor roadshows, board offsites, customer trips, hiring loops, conference circuits. We handle the travel so the meeting is the thing you walked into the room thinking about.

The reality

Your travel year, as it actually looks.

Seed through Series B founders we work with travel between thirty and sixty times a year. A typical month: two cities for customer meetings, a board offsite, an industry conference, a partner trip, and a recruiting visit. The travel itself is a coordination tax — somewhere between four and ten hours of your week, depending on how messy it gets.

We compress that to a five-line email per trip. You tell us the dates and the city. We come back with a routed itinerary, the hotel options at your usual register, the ground, and the calendar holds. You approve in one thread.

Where we earn the fee

The five trips founders fly that justify having us.

01.

The investor roadshow

Five cities in seven days, eight to twelve meetings a day. Backup options on every leg. A car at every curb. A debrief room booked for two hours each evening. We have run this trip thirty times.

02.

The board offsite

Booking a venue your six directors will actually show up to. Rooms blocked at the right register. Dietary handled. Dinner reservation that does not make you look cheap or profligate. Spouses included or not, on your call.

03.

The conference circuit

HLTH, Money 20/20, Slush, Web Summit, Dreamforce. Hotels are booked out before the official block opens. We hold rooms early, at preferred rates, with breakfast and a credit attached. You decide attendance by Tuesday.

04.

The customer trip

You are flying to close. The bag does not get lost. The car does not run late. The hotel is the right register for the dinner. Optionality on the return flight in case the meeting runs long. None of it is your problem.

05.

The hiring loop

You are flying a candidate in. The flight is on us to coordinate. The hotel is walking distance from the office. The candidate's preferences are captured. The travel itself becomes part of the recruiting experience.

06.

The 2am rebook

Newark closes. Your morning meeting is in Chicago. You wake up to a confirmation, not a problem. The Desk worked through the night so you could keep sleeping. Atlas members do this two or three times a year on average.

Mini case study — anonymized

How we handle a Series A founder's roadshow week.

Monday, 09:00 ET

Founder confirms the term sheet conversation. Roadshow is on. Five cities — SF, NY, Boston, Austin, LA — in eight business days. We receive a five-line note in Slack.

Monday, 14:30 ET

Itinerary draft returned. United Polaris on the transcons where it matters, premium economy where it does not. Hotels: 1 Hotel SF, The Mark NYC, XV Beacon Boston, The Driskill Austin, Sunset Tower LA. Each booked through a preferred partner — upgrade, breakfast, $100 credit on every property.

Tuesday, 11:00 ET

Founder approves in one Slack message. We confirm everything within ninety minutes. Calendar holds drop on her calendar. EA copied throughout.

Thursday, 02:14 ET (Day 4 of trip)

Boston morning flight cancelled. Desk catches the IROP alert before the airline texts the founder. By 02:41 ET, a Delta seat is held on the 06:00 with a confirmed gate. The founder learns about both the cancellation and the rebook in a single text at 06:00 wake-up.

Friday, week two, 18:00 ET

Roadshow concludes. The founder closes the round on Monday. We deliver a one-page trip report — every spend item categorized, a CO2 estimate, and a note that two transcons could be downshifted next time without lifestyle impact. Saved her finance lead three hours.
