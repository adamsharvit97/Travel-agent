On commissions

How we earn, in plain language.

Hotel commissions

When you book a hotel through us, the hotel pays our agency a commission — typically 10 to 15 percent of the room rate. This does not change your rate. We will disclose the commission on any specific booking on request. We never accept a commission that is material to a recommendation.

Air commissions

Airlines pay little to no commission to agencies in 2026. Air work is largely a labor cost for us, which is why we charge a $50 ticketing fee on each ticket (waived for Atlas members).

Other supplier income

We work with preferred-partner programs (Virtuoso, FHR, STARS, Privé, and direct programs with brands) that pay us additional consideration for using their platforms. This consideration is what funds the benefits attached to your booking — the upgrade, the breakfast, the credit. You receive the value.

What is the bottom line?

Your fee — Atlas membership or per-trip — is the bulk of what we earn from you. Supplier commissions exist; they are disclosed on request; they do not bias our recommendations. If we cannot make money honestly on your account, we will say so on the intro call.
