Frequently asked

Atlas, in detail.

Why charge a membership fee at all?

Because the alternative is that we earn from the supplier, and you cannot see what we earn. We would rather charge you directly, in a number that is on the public site, than have you wonder whether our recommendation favored you or our commission. The retainer aligns the work.

What if I only travel five times this year?

Atlas Principal pays for itself at roughly twelve to fifteen significant trips a year, depending on hotel mix. Below that, you are better off engaging us per-trip. We will tell you which model fits on the intro call. We do not sell memberships people will not use.

Can I add travelers to my plan mid-year?

Yes. Add a traveler to Atlas Office or Firm at any time. We prorate the addition; the renewal date does not change. Removals take effect at renewal.

What does "named human" actually mean?

Every Atlas account has a primary advisor and a backup, both named. Both are at the office (not a call center, not a contractor). After hours, you reach the Desk — also staffed by named people we work with. You will always know who you are talking to and you will always be able to look them up.

What are Latitude Reports?

A quarterly PDF and CSV delivered to your finance lead. Total spend, breakdown by traveler and category, CO2 estimate using DEFRA methodology, policy exceptions logged, and a note on optimization opportunities for the next quarter. Available on Office and Firm tiers.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel any time before renewal. We do not auto-renew without a written confirmation thirty days prior. There is no cancellation fee.

Do you take commissions from suppliers?

Yes — that is how the global travel industry compensates agencies, and it is disclosed in your traveler agreement. The relevant point is that those commissions do not change your rate or our recommendation. We disclose them on request and we never accept a commission that would be material to a decision we make on your behalf.

Are you a US or Canadian company?

The advisor practice is based in Toronto. We operate under Fora Travel LLC, our IATA-accredited US-registered host agency. Bookings, payments, and traveler agreements run through Fora. We invoice in USD. The public site is intended for US clients.
