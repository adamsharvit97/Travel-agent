The 43rd Parallel

Where business travel becomes a service.

We are not cheaper. Sometimes we are. That is not the point. What you get is three things — rewards leverage, convenience, and a human who picks up at 2am.

I.

Rewards & program leverage

Hotel upgrades, daily breakfast, $100 property credits, late checkout, room category bumps. Through Virtuoso, Four Seasons Preferred, STARS, Hyatt Privé, Belmond Bellini, and Rosewood Elite — programs your booking tool cannot access. The bill is the same. The trip is materially different.

II.

Convenience

One human. One email. One Slack channel. You tell us once that you prefer aisle seats, a hot room, sparkling water on arrival, and a black car at the curb — and that is what shows up, every trip, without you re-stating it. The cognitive load returns to your work.

III.

Service when it breaks

Flight cancelled at 02:00 in Frankfurt. Visa rejected at the gate. Hotel walks you on arrival. A single named human picks up — not a call center, not a chatbot, not a queue. First response within 60 minutes, twenty-four hours a day, every day of the year.

What is Latitude Atlas

An annual retainer for travel that has to work.

Latitude Atlas is our membership tier. You pay an annual fee. In return, planning fees are waived, your service-level guarantees are written down, and there is a named human accountable for everything that leaves your terminal.

Three tiers — one traveler, one office, one firm. All disclosed on the public site. No commissions hidden in your fares.
