A short letter

We are a small office. We work with founders, partners, and principals who travel for the work they care about. We hold the relationship — flights, hotels, cars, the moments that go wrong at 2 a.m. — so the work you flew for is what you remember of the trip.

We do not sell software. We do not run a queue. We do not own a hotel. We are paid by you, and answerable to you, on a relationship that begins with an application and is renewed by results.
