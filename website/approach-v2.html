SLA — Latitude Desk

First response: under 60 minutes. Day or night. 365.

Not "24/7" as a marketing claim. A written commitment, named on your file, escalated if missed.

Written commitment

Three numbers, in writing.

60 min First response, all hours

Email, Slack, or phone — answered by a named human within sixty minutes of arrival. Day or night. Weekends and holidays.

15 min Active disruption response

Flight cancels mid-trip, hotel walks you, visa rejected at the gate — the office is on the rebook within fifteen minutes of being notified.

24 hrs Standard planning turn

For non-urgent itinerary requests, a draft proposal back within one business day. Approve in a single thread. The office confirms.

Your principal

Your principal handles your account. No pod. No queue. No code to enter at 2am.

One named primary, one named backup. Both are at the office — not a contractor, not a call center. After hours the Desk is staffed by people whose names are on your traveler agreement.

What we are not

The three categories the office is not.

Stated plainly so the fit is honest in both directions.

  • Corporate booking tools We are not a policy-enforcement and expense-capture platform. Those tools standardise the bottom half of a travel programme. The office is the top.
  • Card concierges We are not a call centre attached to a charge card. A concierge does not know your file, your principal, or the meeting you are flying into.
  • Leisure agencies We are not a destination-curation practice. The office is built for the trips that are work — with the discretion the work requires.
