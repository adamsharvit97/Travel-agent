Frequently asked

Honest answers to the obvious questions.

The twelve questions we are asked most often. Answered directly, without marketing language. If a question is missing, write to the office — we will add it.

01.

I already have an Amex Platinum concierge. Why do I need Latitude?

The Amex concierge is a benefit, not a service relationship. They are excellent at restaurant reservations and reasonable at hotel bookings inside the Fine Hotels and Resorts collection. They cannot rebook a cancelled international flight at 02:00. They do not hold a profile of your seating, dietary, hotel-floor, or loyalty preferences across years. They do not know your EA. They do not produce a quarterly Latitude Report for your CFO. Amex is a useful card. Latitude is the operating office around the way you actually travel. Most of our members keep both.

02.

I use Navan or TravelPerk for my company. How is this different?

Navan and TravelPerk are booking tools. They are well built for self-serve transactional travel under a corporate policy at scale. Latitude is a service relationship for people whose travel is too high-stakes for self-serve. We are not competing for the same dollars. We layer above them — many of our members keep Navan for the team and use Latitude for the founder, the partners, the principals. When the flight cancels at 02:00, the Navan call centre is a queue. The Desk is a named human who picks up.

03.

Aren't travel advisors more expensive than booking direct?

No. Hotel rates booked through Latitude are the same as the hotel's best public rate — we do not mark them up, and we cannot. What you receive on top is the preferred-partner benefit package: room upgrade subject to availability, daily breakfast for two, a property credit of $100 or more, late checkout. Airfare is the same as the airline's price. Where you do pay extra is the Atlas membership itself — $189 or $349 a month, all trip design included — and the design fees for non-members, all disclosed on the Atlas page. We publish the numbers because that is the only honest way to do this.

04.

What happens if I want to book my own trip sometimes?

Then you book it. Atlas does not require exclusivity. Some members book all of their travel with us; others book weekend leisure trips themselves and bring us the business travel. We do not penalize self-booked trips. We will, however, lose the ability to support a self-booked trip if it goes sideways — if you booked through Expedia and the hotel walks you, we can advocate but we cannot escalate through a partner channel we are not on. Tell us what you want to handle yourself and we will handle the rest.

05.

How does the 60-minute SLA actually work?

Every Atlas account has two named humans during office hours, plus the Latitude Desk after hours, on weekends, and on holidays. During office hours you reach your primary advisor in your channel; if she is in a meeting, the backup picks up. After hours, the Desk picks up — three named advisors who rotate the on-call rotation. First response within sixty minutes, twenty-four hours a day, every day of the year. We log every SLA event. Quarterly reports show our performance against it; we publish that figure to members.

06.

What if you're asleep at 2am Toronto time and my flight cancels?

The Desk is staffed continuously by named humans across three time zones. Your primary advisor sleeps. The Desk does not. When you ping the channel at 02:00 ET, the on-call advisor in a covering time zone picks it up — first response within sixty minutes, usually under twenty. Your primary advisor is briefed the next morning and takes the handoff. You will never reach a call centre, a chatbot, or a queue. The continuity of the relationship is the point of the membership.

07.

How do you handle data security and PII?

Traveler profiles, passport details, and payment information are stored in our encrypted client management system, hosted in SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure through Fora Travel and its providers. We do not share PII with third parties except as required to fulfill a booking — and only with the supplier of record for that booking. We do not sell, rent, or syndicate member data, ever. On request, we can provide a written data-handling memorandum for your CFO or general counsel.

08.

Can I cancel my membership mid-year?

Yes. There is no cancellation fee. Atlas memberships do not auto-renew without a written confirmation thirty days prior to the renewal date. If you cancel mid-year, we honor the remaining months and we will refund any unused planning fees that would have been waived. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you on the intro call before you sign — and on rare occasions we have refunded the first month of a membership when a fit was not what we expected. We would rather you not be a member than be the wrong one.

09.

Do you handle leisure travel too, or just business?

Both. The membership covers everything you travel for — board meetings, sales trips, an anniversary in Florence, a family week in Mallorca, a graduation trip to Tokyo. The same advisor handles both. The same preferred-partner benefits apply. Most of our members find that leisure trips are where the membership feels most luxurious; business trips are where it is most operationally valuable. We are equally engaged in both.

10.

What if I'm not sure I travel enough to justify the membership?

Then engage us per-trip first. Non-member trip design starts at $500; the fee scales with the trip's value and is quoted before the work begins. After one or two trips together you will know whether the relationship is worth $189 a month, and the design fee you paid credits against your first three months of Atlas Light if you join within twelve months. We do not sell memberships people will not use. If your year is two simple trips, per-trip is the right model and we will say so.

11.

How do I refer a friend or colleague?

Send them the public site and let them book the intro call themselves; or, if you prefer, send us their name and we will reach out. We do not pay referral fees, by design — we would rather the recommendation be honest than incentivized. As a thank-you, members whose referrals become Atlas accounts receive a credit equivalent to one month of their tier applied to the next renewal. This is a quiet courtesy, not a marketing program.

12.

I'm in Toronto or Canada. Can I become a member?

The public site is intended for U.S.-based travelers, because our host agency Fora Travel LLC is U.S.-registered and we invoice in USD. We are happy to talk to Canadian residents about per-trip work, particularly cross-border business travel. For Canadian residents wanting full membership, we will be transparent: the regulatory and tax fit is more complex, and in some cases another advisor will be a better match. Send us a note either way; we will tell you honestly.
