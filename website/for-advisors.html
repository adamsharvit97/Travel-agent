Built for · Financial Advisors

Client trips bring assets in. They should not also take a Tuesday out of your week.

For RIA principals, wealth managers, and senior advisors with $100M and up in AUM. We run the travel for client appreciation, prospecting events, and the conferences where your peers decide where you stand.

Why advisors use us

You are in the trust business. Your travel should not be the place that breaks it.

Wealth clients notice. They notice when the car is late, when the wine pairing was lazy, when the hotel concierge did not know they were coming. None of those signal anything about your investment thesis — and all of them speak louder than your last quarterly letter.

We treat client travel as a deliverable. Itineraries are documented, ground is confirmed, room categories are upgraded into the reservation rather than at check-in, and dietary or allergy notes are on file with the property. The client experience is the work product.

Mini case study — anonymized

A $900M RIA's top-client weekend in Napa.

Twelve weeks out

RIA founder decides on a top-twelve client weekend in Napa. We are briefed in a thirty-minute call. Preferences for each couple — dietary, sleep, wine, mobility — are captured in a shared profile sheet.

Ten weeks out

Auberge du Soleil holds twelve rooms in suite categories. Booked through Virtuoso — upgrade at check-in, breakfast for two each morning, $100 credit per stay. Private dinner at Press confirmed. Saturday Inglenook visit set.

Three weeks out

Each couple receives a personalized welcome card with the weekend's itinerary, ground details, and a direct number for the Desk if something is needed in real time.

Friday afternoon

Couple seven's flight from Chicago is diverted to Sacramento. The Desk catches the alert, rebooks them onto a later SFO flight, and reroutes their car to meet them. They arrive at Auberge thirty minutes behind schedule — well in time for the welcome reception.

Monday morning

Client onboardings begin Tuesday. Two of the twelve couples introduce children's accounts to the firm within ninety days. The RIA renews Atlas Firm and asks us to plan three more weekends in the calendar year.
