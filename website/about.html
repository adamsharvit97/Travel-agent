Principles

How the office works.

Five things we believe, applied to every booking we make.

I.

Disclosed pricing, always.

What we charge is on the public site. If we earn a commission from a supplier, we will tell you on request. There is no scenario in which our compensation is hidden in your rate.

II.

One human, one file.

Every account has a named primary advisor and a named backup. You will not be handed off to a call center. The person who knows your file is the person who picks up.

III.

Service-level guarantees in writing.

Sixty-minute first response, twenty-four hours a day. Fifteen-minute response to active disruption. Twenty-four-hour turn on standard planning. These are not aspirations — they are commitments.

IV.

Small book. Long relationships.

Each advisor at the office holds a finite number of accounts. We would rather decline new work than dilute the existing relationships. The intro call is a fit conversation in both directions.

V.

Quiet, accountable work.

We do not post our client list. We do not name names. We do not use testimonials with first-name-only attribution. The work is the marketing. Referrals from clients are the only growth channel we plan to need.

VI.

The honest "no."

If Atlas is not right for you, we will say so on the intro call. We will recommend an alternative — a different advisor, a different model, or no advisor at all. We are not building this office on memberships people do not need.

Discipline

What we do not do.

The negative list matters as much as the affirmative one. Here is what an engagement with Latitude 43 will never include.

  • We do not book leisure-only travel as a primary line of business. Atlas members can use the office for personal trips; the office is not built around them.
  • We do not work with Fortune 500 corporate travel programs. Our work is for the small fund, the founding team, the solo operator, the principal.
  • We do not pitch destinations. We do not sell experiences. We do not produce vacation itineraries with a flourish. We book travel.
  • We do not market to Canadian residents on the public site. Our regulatory authority for retail Canadian travel sits with a different body. US clients only.
  • We do not accept supplier commissions that would meaningfully bias a recommendation. When we cannot disclose a commission, we decline the booking.
  • We do not run a 1-800 number. The Desk is reached by direct line, email, or Slack — the way a colleague would reach you.
  • We do not use stock photography of palm trees, cruise ships, or family vacations. This is not what the work looks like.
