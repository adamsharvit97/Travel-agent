Deal travel, LP travel, and the trips you cannot afford to look tired for.

For partners at venture funds under $2B AUM, and PE deal teams of any size. LP meetings, diligence trips, portfolio company visits, AGMs, and the off-cycle conference where the next allocation gets decided.

Why funds use us

You are flying the register of the LP, not the associate.

Funds under $2B AUM cannot justify an in-house travel manager. The IR head books their own. The partners book their own. The deal team books last and worst. The result is uneven — the LP meeting in Geneva goes well, the AGM travel goes off the rails.

Atlas Office and Atlas Firm give a fund a single travel desk for all of the above, at a flat cost. The reporting goes to the CFO. The Desk goes to the partners. The deal team gets booked the way the partners get booked. The register holds.

The travel that matters

What we run for funds.

01.

LP fundraising travel

Geneva, Zurich, London, Singapore, Riyadh. The trips where the next $50M is decided. We hold backup options on every flight, the hotel is the LP's preferred property when we can determine it, and the ground is a black car — never a rideshare.

02.

Diligence trips

Two days, three cities, often confirmed Monday for Wednesday. We hold optionality. Hotel cancellation windows are tracked. We unwind a trip with one Slack message when the deal dies on Tuesday afternoon.

03.

Portfolio company visits

Three companies in two days, rotating across the partnership. We coordinate ground between offices, book a working lunch that the founders will not have to organize, and route around the partner's known dietary and schedule preferences.

04.

AGM and annual meeting

For the fund's annual meeting, we coordinate LP arrival logistics — flights, hotel, ground, a welcome packet at check-in. The LP feels managed. The IR head gets two days back.

05.

Sourcing conference travel

SuperReturn, IPEM, Web Summit, Bloomberg Invest. Hotels block out at the conference rate before the wave hits. We block early, often months in advance, at preferred rates with full benefits.

06.

The crisis trip

A portfolio company is going sideways. You need to be in San Francisco tomorrow morning. We book the redeye, hold a room at the Battery for arrival, and have a car at SFO before you land. The Desk does this in under an hour.

Mini case study — anonymized

LP travel for a $400M fund's mid-cycle close.

Eight weeks out

The fund's IR head shares a target LP list with us. We map travel hubs and propose a routing — three Europe legs over two weeks, with a fund-day in London anchoring the trip.

Six weeks out

Air booked in Delta One and Air France La Première where the schedule cooperates. Hotels held: Connaught London, Le Bristol Paris, Park Hyatt Zurich. Each booked through a preferred program with upgrade and breakfast attached.

London day

We arrange a private dining room at a club the IR head selects, transport from each LP's office, and a 4pm car back to Heathrow for the senior partner. The day runs to the minute.

Day after the trip

Latitude Reports drops a trip summary into the CFO's inbox. Total spend by leg, by traveler, by LP-touch, with CO2 estimates and a note on which preferred-partner benefit cleared on each stay.

Six months later

Fund closes oversubscribed. The IR head writes the partners that the travel was the smoothest she has run. The CFO renews Atlas Office without negotiation.
