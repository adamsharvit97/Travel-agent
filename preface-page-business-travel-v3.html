FAQ

Things people ask before signing up. Honest answers.

Do I lose my Marriott / Hyatt / Hilton points?
No. I book under your loyalty number, your card on file. You earn the same points as if you booked direct. The Privé / STARS / Impresario amenity package layers on top of your existing status — it doesn't replace it.
Can you book through my company's TMS (Concur, Egencia, TripActions)?
No. This is a personal-account model. If your company has a corporate travel program, that's a different lane. Preface is for founders, partners, and sales execs whose travel is on their own card.
What hotels are covered?
All Marriott luxury & lifestyle (Ritz, St. Regis, Edition, JW, W) via STARS. All Hyatt brands (Park Hyatt, Andaz, Alila, Miraval) via Privé. Hilton's luxury portfolio (Waldorf, Conrad, LXR) via for Luxury. Plus FSPP, Belmond Bellini Club, Rosewood Elite, Mandarin Oriental Fan Club. About 2,500+ properties globally.
What about budget hotels (Hampton, Courtyard)?
These aren't in the amenity programs — booking them through me gets you the same room as Booking.com with no perks. Book Hampton yourself, send me the luxury and full-service brands.
What's the catch?
Standard tier truly has no catch — Fora (my host agency) earns commission from the hotel out of its marketing budget. The same commission Booking.com keeps for itself. I'd rather make the commission delivering you upgrades than have a faceless OTA keep it.
How do you handle flights?
Flight booking is Concierge tier and up. Through Fora's Flights Desk I can book published-fare tickets at the same rate as Expedia, plus access to some negotiated business-class fares (typically 10-20% off published business class on international routes). Status credits accrue to your accounts.
What's the minimum commitment?
Standard: none. Concierge: month-to-month, cancel anytime. Executive: month-to-month, cancel anytime. No annual contracts.
