## The offer

A members' travel office. One named advisor per member, 60-minute first-response SLA in writing, 24/7/365. B2B booking channels the prospect cannot reach themselves: Virtuoso, Amex FHR, Four Seasons Preferred Partner (STARS). The EA stays on the calendar; the desk takes the airline calls and the 11 pm reroutes. The pitch in one sentence: "Light $189 a month, one traveler. Office $349 a month, up to five travelers and your EA, everything included." First Trip Guarantee: if the first trip fails on our end, the month back, the next month free, and a service credit. Application-based; roughly two of five applications declined.

## Audience types

### Founder / CEO, seed–Series B
- **Pain:** founder or co-founder spends 80–200 hours/year on travel logistics, or an EA inherits it and loses the hours twice over.
- **Recommended tier:** Light solo; Office the moment a co-founder, exec, or EA travels. Bootstrapped founders: the spouse line — "$189 is not really for you. It is for Sunday night with your partner."
- **Triggers:** funding round close, EA hire, conference circuit, investor roadshows.

### VC / PE partner or principal
- **Pain:** LP roadshows, portfolio visits, and the conference circuit run on calendar-and-Concur; the friction shows at the gate.
- **Recommended tier:** Office Unlimited covering the partnership; Light for an individual partner.
- **Triggers:** fund close, LP roadshow, AGM season, conference season (SOSV, Upfront, Future Proof).

### RIA / wealth management principal, $100M+ AUM
- **Pain:** 60+ road days/year between client visits, Schwab Impact, Future Proof; every trip is a brand expression.
- **Recommended tier:** Light solo or Office for a partnership.
- **Triggers:** AUM milestones, M&A, podcast/press appearances, conference season.

### Family office / HNW principal
- **Pain:** complex multi-traveler itineraries, family trips with zero failure tolerance; aviation and properties handled, commercial travel under-served.
- **Recommended tier:** Family Office variant. Genuinely HNW: disqualify up to Society — "$349 is the wrong product for you."
- **Triggers:** new family-office hire, relocation, school-calendar patterns, board seats.

## Voice rules
- **Do:** say "the desk", "member", "apply" — not "the team", "customer", "sign up". Short sentences. Periods. Name things precisely ("AC 704", not "the morning flight"). Numbers with units ("60 minutes", "$349 a month").
- **Don't:** no exclamation marks, no emojis, no adverbs of intensity (truly, genuinely, incredibly, absolutely). Banned: bespoke, curated, luxe, wanderlust, journey (noun), experience (noun), unforgettable, magical, dream, excited, thrilled, reach out, circle back, touch base, hey there, don't miss out, limited time.
- **The test:** if it could appear in a private bank's quarterly letter, it is on-brand; if it could appear in a travel agency's Instagram caption, it is not.

## Objection bank
- **"How is this different from my Amex Platinum concierge?"** → Amex Platinum is an outsourced call center (Ten Lifestyle Group / Aspire); Centurion's dedicated model was downgraded ~2022; email concierge eliminated 2021, off-list reservations 2022. We are a named human, salaried, fifteen members maximum, on a published 60-minute SLA.
- **"How is this different from Navan / TravelPerk?"** → Different category. Navan is software for a finance team's dashboard; its support is a chat queue. We operate in the layer above it — some members keep Navan for company spend and use Latitude for the trips that matter.
- **"My EA already handles this."** → Your EA is a generalist; travel is a 10,000-hour specialty. We partner WITH the EA: their travel hours drop by about half and trip quality rises because we hold upgrade programs they can't access. The EA becomes a fan first.
- **"That's a lot of money for twenty flights a year."** → Walk the math at their tier. At ~$80K annual spend we typically capture $3,000–9,000 in upgrades, credits, and rebooked savings against $2,268–4,188 a year — but honestly the buy is never thinking about it again, like the ROI of your accountant. If they balk at $349, reprice their last anniversary trip at agency design rates.
- **"What's the all-in?"** → One number. $189 or $349 a month. All trip design included, business and personal. No per-trip fees, no surprise invoices.
- **"What if I want to book my own trip?"** → Book it. Forward the confirmation; we attach benefits after the fact and watch for IROPs. You pay for the desk to be there, not to be required.
- **"How do I know you won't disappear in six months?"** → Hosted under Fora Travel, so the regulatory and supplier infrastructure outlives any one advisor. References available before signing.
- **"Can I cancel mid-year?"** → Month to month. No annual commitment.
- **"Do you handle leisure travel?"** → Yes, included for members. Work-first, life-aware — the anniversary in Lisbon and the board meeting in Geneva go through the same desk.

## Cadence rules
- **First touch:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 9:00–10:30 a.m. prospect-local.
- **Follow-up:** +5 business days, one paragraph, no second pitch — a single specific insight tied to the prospect's published activity.
- **Stop rule:** never past two touches without a referral or a fresh signal.

## Anti-pitch notes
Never discount the headline price — concede structure instead (First Trip Guarantee, pilot, EA seat). Never sell Society off a call; it is a disqualify-up only. The selling motion is quiet and specific, never loud.
