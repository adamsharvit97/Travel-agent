Chapter V · A recent booking

One trip. Recently.

Last March, a couple booked their honeymoon to Six Senses Fiji. They paid $8,000 for what they thought was a Pool Villa. At check-in, the front desk handed them keys to the Premier Beachfront Pool Villa.

The room they walked into would have cost $2,400 more on Booking.com.

Daily breakfast was included. A $100 spa credit waited at the concierge desk. A bottle of champagne sat on the table, with a note: "Mr. & Mrs. K. — welcome home."

None of it cost extra.
None of it was asked for.

The hotel paid Fora the commission they would have paid Booking.com. Except Booking.com would have kept it. We hand it back to clients in the room.
