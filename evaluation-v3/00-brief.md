## Output requirements

- **Format:** clean markdown, no HTML, no brand styling, no Chart.js
- **Length:** 3,500–5,000 words. Substantive depth, not padding.
- **Voice:** McKinsey-trained operator. Honest. No sycophancy. Called grades.
- **Structure:**
  1. One-paragraph verdict at top (grade + headline finding)
  2. What's right (1,000–1,500 words, specific, with citations)
  3. What's questionable (1,000–1,500 words, specific risks)
  4. What's wrong or missing (500–1,000 words)
  5. Recommendations (5–10 numbered, executable, prioritized)
- **Banned words:** luxury, bespoke, curated, white-glove, seamless, elevated, wanderlust, concierge
- **No exclamation marks. Specific dollars and minutes, not "approximately."**
