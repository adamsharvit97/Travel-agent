FAQ

Asked before signing up.

Do I lose my points or status?

No. Bookings carry your loyalty number and your card. Points and elite nights accrue exactly as if you booked direct.

What's the catch on the free tier?

Hotels pay advisors out of their marketing budget — the same line that funds the booking sites. Your rate doesn't move. Happy to prove it on a live booking.

Can this replace my company's Concur?

It's a personal-account model, not a TMS. Most clients run hotels through me, mandated tools for the rest — and call me when the mandate fails at 11pm.

Which hotels count?

Marriott STARS, Hyatt Privé, Hilton Impresario, Four Seasons Preferred, Rosewood Elite, Virtuoso — ~4,500 properties. Anything else I'll still book; you just get me instead of perks.

Minimum commitment?

None on Standard; paid tiers are month-to-month. If I stop being useful, fire me by text.

What about flights only?

Flights live in Concierge and up — fare classes that protect your status, seats that protect your sleep, and rebooking handled when things break.

25+ nights a year? You're leaving ~$4K in the lobby.

Twenty minutes. I'll look at your current travel pattern and show you exactly what the desk would have added to your last quarter. If the math doesn't impress you, we shake hands and you keep booking direct.
