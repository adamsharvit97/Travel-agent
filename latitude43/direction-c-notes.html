How it works

Three steps. The first one's free.

1.We talk for twenty minutes

Who's going, when, and what "a great trip" means to you — beach-and-a-book or three-reservations-a-day. I'll tell you honestly if I'm useful for it.

2.You get one clean plan

Flights, rooms, transfers, the table by the window, the day left deliberately empty. Booked and confirmed, with every perk my hotel relationships carry.

3.I'm on call while you're away

Cancelled flight at 11 pm? You text one person, then go find the lounge. Rebooking is my problem now — that's the entire point of having me.
