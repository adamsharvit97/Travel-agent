A person, when it goes sideways

Cancelled flight, missed connection, room that isn't what the photos said. You text me. It becomes my problem while you order another drink.

The fine print, printed large

You don't pay me. The hotels do.

Hotels pay advisors a commission for bringing them good guests — it's how the industry has worked for decades. You pay the same rate you'd find yourself, and usually walk away with more attached to the booking.

Most trips: no planning fee. Complex multi-stop itineraries carry a flat fee, quoted before any work starts. That's the entire pricing page.
