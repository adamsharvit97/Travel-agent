Dispatches

One Toronto founder. 32 nights. A quarter audited.

The client — a founder who flies to wherever his customers are, which last quarter meant eight trips across six cities — used to book everything himself. Not badly, either. He had status, he knew his properties, he could find the corporate rate.

What he couldn't do was clear a suite upgrade at booking, get the breakfast comp'd through STARS, or have someone else stand in the (figurative) rebooking queue when two of those thirty-two nights fell apart at the airport. Onboarding took twenty minutes in January.

Every booking since has been a two-line text, answered inside the hour, charged to his own card, points to his own account. The audit at right is his quarter — names changed, numbers not.

The cost over booking direct: zero dollars. The desk is paid by the hotels' marketing budgets — the same line that pays the booking sites.

Letters to the desk

Asked, frequently. Answered, honestly.

surely I lose my Marriott points?

You don't. Bookings carry your loyalty number and your card; points and elite nights accrue exactly as if you'd booked direct. The perks stack on top.

what, then, is the catch?

Hotels pay advisors from their marketing budget — the same budget that pays the booking sites their 15–20%. Your rate does not move. I'll happily prove it on a live booking.

my company makes me use Concur.

Condolences. This is a personal-account model, not a TMS — most clients run hotels through the desk, the mandate for flights, and text me when the mandate fails them at 11pm.

which hotels are covered?

Marriott STARS, Hyatt Privé, Hilton Impresario, Four Seasons Preferred, Rosewood Elite, and the Virtuoso network — roughly 4,500 properties. Anything else I'll still book; you simply get me instead of perks.

and the minimum commitment?

None on Standard; the paid tiers run month-to-month. If the desk stops being useful, fire it by text. It will take the news professionally.

do you do the odd holiday too?

Quietly, yes — Executive includes the family's leisure trips, and Standard clients get first call when the honeymoon-grade properties release their good weeks.

— STOP PRESS —

Travel 25+ nights a year? The desk will pay for itself by Tuesday.

Twenty minutes on the phone. Bring your last quarter's travel; I'll show you, line by line, what the desk would have added to it. If the numbers bore you, no hard feelings — you keep the audit.
