Before you ask

Things people ask before signing up.

Q.Do I lose my Marriott / Hyatt / Hilton points?

No. Bookings go under your loyalty number with your card. Points and status accrue exactly as if you booked direct — the perks stack on top, they don't replace anything.

Q.What's the catch?

Standard tier genuinely has no catch: hotels pay advisors a commission out of their marketing budget, the same line item that funds Booking.com's fees. Your rate doesn't move. I'd rather show you that on a live booking than argue it here.

Q.Can my company book through you instead of a TMS?

This is a personal-account model, not Concur. If your company mandates a corporate tool, I work alongside it — most clients run hotels through me and flights through the mandate, then call me when the mandate fails them at 11pm.

Q.Which hotels are covered?

Marriott (STARS & Luminous), Hyatt Privé, Hilton Impresario, Four Seasons Preferred Partner, Rosewood Elite, Belmond Bellini, and the Virtuoso network — about 4,500 properties. Outside the programs I still book it; you just get me instead of perks.

Q.What's the minimum commitment?

None on Standard. Concierge and Executive are month-to-month, cancel anytime. If I stop being useful, fire me by text.
