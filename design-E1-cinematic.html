— What it looks like in practice

One booking. Recently.

Last month, a couple booked their honeymoon to Six Senses Fiji. They paid $8,000 for what they thought was a Pool Villa. At check-in, the front desk handed them keys to the Premier Beachfront Pool Villa.

The room they walked into would have cost $2,400 more on Booking.com.

Daily breakfast was included. A $100 spa credit waited at the concierge desk. A bottle of champagne sat on the table, with a note: "Mr. & Mrs. K. — Welcome home."

None of it cost extra. None of it was asked for. The hotel paid Fora the commission they would have paid Booking.com — except Booking.com would have kept it.
